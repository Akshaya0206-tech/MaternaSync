THE AI CARE ASSISTANT  (system behind the doctor's Care Assistant tab, all [B])
=====================================================================================
  Code: backend/app/ai_coordinator/ (LangGraph graphs + policy engine + 7 registered tools)
  What it does
    - New upload: checks name, record number, date, duplicates. Only PROPOSES verify/reject.
      Looks like someone else's, or says it is urgent -> handed to the doctor.
    - New question: urgent wording -> fixed emergency message in her language at once + urgent
      alert to the doctor. Clinical/unclear -> assigned to a doctor, NO draft answer.
      Administrative (next appointment, how to upload) -> fixed-wording proposed reply.
    - Missed check-in: one fixed reminder a day to her and to trusted people she allowed;
      after 3, asks a doctor to look. Wording says a missing entry is not a sign of anything.
    - Task overdue 3+ days: raised to the doctor once.
  What it will NEVER do: diagnose, advise on treatment, answer a clinical question, interpret a
  document, release anything to a patient without a doctor, or act on another patient's data.
  Guards: modes off / shadow (records only) / assist (no autonomous mode); kill switch read on
  every step; same-patient and recipient checks; each step only once; daily cap per patient;
  every stopped or failed step becomes an item for the doctor; every step logged with mode,
  provider, prompt version. Its own account cannot sign in.
  Model step: default "rules" (plain code, nothing leaves the server). Optional Claude provider
  (MATERNASYNC_AI_PROVIDER=claude): names/record numbers removed first; the more cautious of
  model and fixed word-lists wins. NOT yet tested against the live Claude API.
  Runs in the background: queue every 5 seconds, follow-up checks every 30 minutes.

  MATERNASYNC_CARE_TEAM_ENABLED    default false. true = care-team login back on.
  MATERNASYNC_AI_COORDINATOR_MODE  off | shadow | assist (default assist). Also the kill switch.
  MATERNASYNC_AI_PROVIDER          rules (default) | claude
  MATERNASYNC_AI_MODEL             for claude; default claude-opus-5-5 (claude-haiku-5-5 is cheaper)
  MATERNASYNC_AI_WORKER            background job on/off, default true
  MATERNASYNC_AI_DAILY_CAP         assistant steps per patient per day, default 40
  (Older flags unchanged: MATERNASYNC_HANDOVER_PAUSED, _DEMO_ENABLED, _WHATSAPP_*, etc.)

KNOWN GAPS / THINGS THE TEAM SHOULD KNOW
=====================================================================================
  - NOT BUILT as assistant workflows: scheduling, referrals, WhatsApp outreach, paperwork drafts,
    outcomes reporting, handover. No autonomous mode. No per-step undo. No evaluation set yet.
  - The default demo "AI" is rules-based. The Claude provider is written but untested live. If
    used for real, names are removed but document text is still sent: needs a data agreement.
  - Hindi / Tamil / Telugu text (about 100 new strings) was written by Claude and needs a native
    speaker's review. Sign-in, sign-up and doctor screens are English only.
  - A caregiver needs an email + password. No SMS/WhatsApp alerts to caregivers.
  - A caregiver's question and its reply are visible to the caregiver and the doctor; the woman
    sees it under Profile -> Trusted people -> "See what they did".
  - First download grew about 8 kB (sign-in about 133 kB compressed) because translations are bundled.
  - Still missing against the hackathon rules: a 60-90 day impact measurement plan, and light
    integration with existing systems (the app is standalone).
  - Nearest item to the "no interpretation / no decision support" line: ICD-10 candidate codes
    (phrase matching on the doctor's own note; the doctor ticks each code).
  - All [B] work is uncommitted. Ask Andry to commit and push before you rely on it.
