THE AI CARE ASSISTANT  (system behind the doctor's Care Assistant tab, all [B])
=====================================================================================
  Code: backend/app/ai_coordinator/ (LangGraph graphs + policy engine + 7 registered tools)
  What it does
    - New upload: checks name, record number, date, duplicates. Only PROPOSES verify/reject.
      Looks like someone else's, or says it is urgent -> handed to the doctor.
    - New question: urgent wording -> fixed emergency message in her language at once + urgent
      alert to the doctor. Clinical/unclear -> assigned to a doctor, NO draft answer.
      Administrative (next appointment, how to upload) -> fixed-wording proposed reply.
    - Missed check-in: one fixed reminder a day to her and to trusted people she allowed;
      after 3, asks a doctor to look. Wording says a missing entry is not a sign of anything.
    - Task overdue 3+ days: raised to the doctor once.
  What it will NEVER do: diagnose, advise on treatment, answer a clinical question, interpret a
  document, release anything to a patient without a doctor, or act on another patient's data.
  Guards: modes off / shadow (records only) / assist (no autonomous mode); kill switch read on
  every step; same-patient and recipient checks; each step only once; daily cap per patient;
  every stopped or failed step becomes an item for the doctor; every step logged with mode,
  provider, prompt version. Its own account cannot sign in.
  Model step: default "rules" (plain code, nothing leaves the server). Optional Claude provider
  (MATERNASYNC_AI_PROVIDER=claude): names/record numbers removed first; the more cautious of
  model and fixed word-lists wins. NOT yet tested against the live Claude API.
  Runs in the background: queue every 5 seconds, follow-up checks every 30 minutes.

HUMAN OVERRIDE (Care Assistant tab -> Your controls, and the log below it, all [B])
=====================================================================================
  Undo     Every finished step that can be reversed has an Undo button in "What the assistant did".
           It says what will change first, asks for an optional reason, and is logged. It undoes:
             - a reminder or the fixed emergency message: withdrawn from her Updates
             - an assignment of a question: put back as it was
             - a verified or rejected document: back under review (and gone from her journey)
             - a sent reply: withdrawn, question open again
             - a hand-off to the doctor: dismissed
           It refuses when the item has moved on since (answered, reassigned, document changed).
           Proposals a doctor approved can be undone too. Steps from before this change cannot.
  Pause    Pause for everyone, or for one patient. Work stays queued and runs when resumed.
           While paused: every new question is still handed to the doctor by a fixed rule, and a
           question with a warning sign still gets the fixed emergency message (the one thing a
           pause never delays; the doctor can still withdraw it).
  Review   "Ask me to approve every step first": reminders wait as proposals. Handing a question to a
           doctor and the emergency message are never held back.
  Safety   If the assistant is switched off, in shadow mode, paused or fails, a new question is
  net      assigned to the patient's doctor and an upload is notified to the doctor by a fixed rule,
           so nothing becomes invisible (the doctor's question list only shows assigned questions).
  Code     ai_coordinator/controls.py, tools.undo, routers/assistant.py (settings, patients/.../pause,
           actions/.../undo). New table assistant_settings; new columns on ai_actions are added
           automatically at start.

KNOWN GAPS / THINGS THE TEAM SHOULD KNOW
=====================================================================================
  - NOT BUILT as assistant workflows: scheduling, referrals, WhatsApp outreach, paperwork drafts,
    outcomes reporting, handover. No autonomous mode. No evaluation set yet.
  - The default demo "AI" is rules-based. The Claude provider is written but untested live. If
    used for real, names are removed but document text is still sent: needs a data agreement.
  - Still open on the assistant: the W4 daily digest, reassigning work when an owner is away,
    drafts of insurance forms / certificates / discharge summaries, and any outcomes read from
    documents (left out on purpose: that would interpret clinical documents). Nothing has been
    run against a real WhatsApp account or a real hospital.
  - Hindi / Tamil / Telugu text (about 100 new strings) was written by Claude and needs a native
    speaker's review: about 1,100 new strings across sign-in and the doctor app, written by Claude.
    A few terms differ between screens (for example Tamil "Follow-up" is one word on the doctor
    screens and another on the patient screens).
  - Not translated, on purpose: names, notes, transcripts, reference documents and publisher names,
    the details inside a Care Assistant item (names, file names, times), and the care-team screens
    (that login is off). Updates, WhatsApp reminders and handover drafts ARE translated now.
  - A caregiver needs an email + password. No SMS/WhatsApp alerts to caregivers.
  - A caregiver's question and its reply are visible to the caregiver and the doctor; the woman
    sees it under Profile -> Trusted people -> "See what they did".
  - First download grew about 8 kB (sign-in about 133 kB compressed) because translations are bundled.
  - Still missing against the hackathon rules: a 60-90 day impact measurement plan, and light
    integration with existing systems (the app is standalone).
  - "No interpretation" is now covered by design (nothing reads a note or labels a value) and by the
    guard tests, but the tests check wording and structure only; they cannot prove the absence of
    every possible interpretation. Reference-document category labels "High Risk" / "High-risk
    pregnancy" remain (they group guideline documents and grade no patient).
  - The 84 "About lab tests" notes and the new Hindi/Tamil/Telugu wording need a native speaker's and
    a doctor's review before real use. Until a doctor approves them, lab explanations have no
    general test descriptions at all.
  - All [B] work is uncommitted. Ask Andry to commit and push before you rely on it.
